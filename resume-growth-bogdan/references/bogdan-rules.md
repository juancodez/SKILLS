# Bogdan Zlatkov — Resume Rules (Source of Truth)

Extracted from "5 Resume Improvements in 5 Minutes with Bogdan Zlatkov" (2026-08-20).
Every rule below is what the evaluator MUST enforce. Do not invent extra rules; do not soften these.

---

## 0. Mindset (frame everything against this)

- Recruiters spend **10–30 seconds** on a resume (not 60).
- It is not about being the best. It is about **standing out from the rest**.
- The resume is the **movie trailer**, not the entire movie.
- Show your **full range** of experience, not your entire experience.

---

## 1. Resume Hook (the top summary block)

A hook has **three required components**. If any one is missing, the hook fails.

| Component | Definition | Fail signal |
|---|---|---|
| **Original** | Few other candidates could truthfully write this line. | Interchangeable across candidates. |
| **Value-add** | Introduces information not already visible elsewhere on the resume. | Restates job title + years already shown. |
| **Informal** | Breaks formality — a human, personal, unexpected line. Critical in the AI era. | Reads like AI/LinkedIn boilerplate. |

**Bonus lift:** Include a **dollar figure** in the hook.
- No dollar figure in hook → ~4% interview rate.
- Dollar figure in hook → ~7% interview rate.

**Banned phrases** (unprovable soft-skill filler — recruiters ignore them):
- "self-starter", "self-driven", "self-disciplined"
- "detail-oriented"
- "mission-driven"
- "results-oriented", "results-driven"
- "hard-working", "passionate", "team player"
- "experienced [role] with X years of experience" (already visible on the resume)

**Reference hook that works:**
> "Content leader, digital marketer, writer. Worked at Emmy award-winning production studio. Beat Mark Zuckerberg at hockey."

---

## 2. Bullet Point Formula

Every bullet must be: **Power Verb + Method/Skill + Result**.

- Canonical order: `Improved SEO strategy through a content audit, resulting in a 23% increase in website traffic.`
- Variant order (result first, for rhythm): `Increased website traffic by 23% by improving SEO strategy through a site-wide content audit.`

**Formatting:**
- **Bold the numbers** so recruiters find them at a glance.
- Do NOT bold whole phrases — only numeric results.

---

## 3. Resume Length

Word count matters, not page count (page count is easy to manipulate).

| Years of experience | Optimal | Absolute max |
|---|---|---|
| 1–15 years | 350–500 words | 500 |
| 15+ years | up to 650 words | 650 |

Exceptions: Australia and some European markets tolerate longer resumes.

Over-length penalty: long resumes go into the **maybe pile** — which, in this market, means no interview.

---

## 4. The "and" Test

- Use Ctrl+F / Cmd+F for the word **and** (also **as well as**).
- **Maximum one "and" per bullet.**
- More than one "and" = the bullet is over-written and must be reduced.

---

## 5. Five Bullet Types — Gap Analysis (color-code)

Classify every bullet into one of these five verb families. A balanced resume shows all five; over-indexing in one family and missing another is the most common failure Bogdan sees.

| Type | Synonyms include | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| **Built** | created, developed, designed, established, initiated, launched | standard |
| **Led** | led, directed, drove, spearheaded, owned | **spotlight** |
| **Managed** | managed, oversaw, coordinated, ran, administered | standard |
| **Collaborated** | collaborated, partnered, worked with, cross-functional, aligned | **spotlight** |
| **Improved** | improved, optimized, increased, reduced, accelerated, doubled | standard |

**Distinction — Managed vs Led:**
- **Managed** = a thing (program, project, budget).
- **Led** = people / direction / initiative (team, direct reports, strategy).

**Spotlight families — Led and Collaborated.** Bogdan calls these out by name in the transcription:
- *"Leadership is obviously a very important quality right now."*
- *"One of the most important soft skills that a recruiter is looking for is someone who can collaborate with different teams."*

Both case studies in the talk (Eric, the marketing client) are built on the same failure: strong Built + Managed, **zero Led or zero Collaborated**. Fixing that gap is presented as the single most impactful edit in the whole workshop.

**Hard flags (auto-deduct — do not average away):**
- 0 Collaborated bullets → recruiters read "lone wolf". Flag even if all other 4 families are present.
- 0 Led bullets on any resume claiming seniority, PM/design lead, ownership, or direct reports → flag as leadership gap.
- Led OR Collaborated < 15% of bullets → still a flag, softer.
- Built + Managed together > 60% with weak Led/Collaborated → "does the work but doesn't lead or partner" — Bogdan's exact failure pattern.

**Failure pattern to flag:** heavy on Built + Led, zero Collaborated → lone wolf. Heavy on Built + Managed, zero Led → individual contributor stuck in place.

## 6. ATS & Keywords

- ATS is **not true AI**. It uses simple keyword-matching algorithms.
- Recruiters search ATS **like Google** — keywords and filters.
- Every ATS grades differently. Adapt per system.
  - Fortune 500 dominated by **Workday** + **SuccessFactors** (~50%).
  - General market dominated by **Greenhouse** + **Lever**.
  - Greenhouse-specific: exact keyword matching, **no benefit from repetition**, recruiters use manual scorecards.
- Include **both** the acronym and the full term (e.g. `SEO` AND `Search Engine Optimization`; `PMP` AND `Project Management Professional`).
- Target a JobScan-style match score of **50–75**. Do **not** chase 100.
- Customization should take **10–15 minutes** per role, not hours.

---

## 7. Application Behavior (context, not resume content — mention only if relevant)

- **Timing beats quality.** 90% of interview-landing candidates applied within the first 24 hours.
- LinkedIn interview rate is ~3%. Diversify: Google Jobs 7%, Wellfound 5%, Glassdoor 3%.
- Tailoring lifts interview rate from 2% → 4%. Worth 10–15 minutes; not worth hours.

---

## Evaluation Rubric (scoring)

Score each block 0–10. Total /70.

| # | Block | 10 = ideal | 0 = fails |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hook — 3 components | Original + value-add + informal all present | Missing any one |
| 2 | Hook — dollar figure | Present | Absent (and role type allows one) |
| 3 | Bullet formula | Every bullet = verb + method + result | Most bullets missing result |
| 4 | Word count | Inside 350–500 (or 500–650 if 15y+) | Over 650 |
| 5 | "and" test | ≤1 `and` per bullet | Multiple bullets with 2+ `and` |
| 6 | 5 verb-type balance | All 5 families present, none over 40% | Missing 2+ families |
| 7 | ATS keywords (if JD provided) | 50–75% match, acronym+full term | <30% or no full-form pairing |

**Verdict bands:**
- 60–70: Ship it.
- 45–59: Fix the flagged blocks, then ship.
- 30–44: Rewrite hook + bullets before sending.
- <30: Start over from the profile — this resume is hurting the candidate.
